# Personal Agent Digital Footprint Privacy Shaking Skill

Designed for **OpenClaw**, **Hermes**, and autonomous personal AI agents to defend personal digital sovereignty against commercial surveillance capitalism, third-party browser fingerprinting, and non-consensual ad targeting profiles.

## Agent In-Context Capabilities

- **Shannon Identification Entropy Audit**: Quantifies individual entropy bits across Canvas, WebGL, User-Agent, Screen Resolution, AudioBuffer, and Font Enumeration.
- **Global Anonymity Pool Calculation**: Computes exact statistical cohort size $K = 2^{33 - H}$ to measure global distinctiveness among 8.5 billion internet users.
- **Orthogonal Decoy Injection**: Generates realistic, Poisson-dispersed synthetic queries across distinct knowledge domains to degrade tracking cluster confidence.

## Tools Summary

1. `audit_fingerprint_entropy`: Computes total bits of identification entropy and recommends specific fingerprint spoofing mitigations.
2. `generate_decoy_traffic_schedule`: Formulates a randomized daily schedule of benign noise searches to dismantle tracking confidence.
